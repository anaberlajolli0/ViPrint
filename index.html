About us

Why ViPrint?

We are the most innovative printing press not only in Kosovo, but across the wider region.

We provide personal attention to detail and follow-up, communicating to you the progress or challenges in meeting your due date. We believe in keeping our promises, and we are committed to standing behind our work.

Realizing how important deadlines are to our customers, we never take on jobs unless we are sure we can complete them on time. We value your time, and offer your business the convenience of pickup and delivery.

Would you like to deal with just one printing house that meets all your needs? From filming, printing, lamination and die cutting to special effects and much more — you'll find every service at ViPrint.

ViPrint is intended to serve as your long-term partner — supporting and meeting your needs through a wide range of products designed specifically for you.

From simple one- or two-color jobs to high-end full-color commercial offset printing, our quality is exceptional. We have the knowledge and experience to complete your projects correctly — the first time.

Technological advantages we possess

Sophisticated machinery

Owning the most modern printing machinery gives ViPrint an edge over the competition — an edge you experience the moment you use the product. In December 2016, ViPrint put into service a B1-format (100×70 cm) offset press from the renowned German company Man Roland, together with other paper and cardboard processing machines.

  • Man Roland 305 — five-color B2 offset press
  • Man Roland 705 — five-color B1 offset press
  • Man Miller TP4 — two-color offset press
  • Man Roland 201 — one-color offset press
  • Heidelberg GTO — one-color offset press
  • Cron-Kodak — computer-to-plate system
  • MBO 66 / 54 — paper folding machines
  • Miller Martin & Heidelberg ST300 — stitching & cutting
  • Horizon — book binding
